$ llm-capacity-planner zai-org/GLM-5.3 --tokens 128k

Summary
  Recommendation: 1 server with 4× NVIDIA B300 SXM serves 50 users guaranteed, 90 likely, with requests of up to 128k
      tokens (≈ 98,000 words ≈ 200 pages; 64k on average).
  Alternative: 1 server with 8× NVIDIA H200 SXM 141GB (fits; 40 users guaranteed, 70 likely).
  Not enough: RTX PRO 6000 Server, L4, L40S, A100 and H100, even with 8 GPUs in one server; RTX A6000, RTX 6000 Ada, RTX
      PRO 6000 WS, RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q and consumer cards, even with 4; GH200, even with 2.
  Speed for each user: writing about 30 words per second (15 to 44; 39 tokens/s); with everyone busy the first words
      appear after about 1.6 s, and a full 128k-token request alone is read in about 3.0 s (1 token ≈ 0.75 words).
  Confidence: memory high (±2.6%); writing speed medium (±48%); reading time low, not precise enough to decide (more
      than ±50%); user count medium (±48%). Plan on the guaranteed figures.

Technical detail
GLM-5.3 at 128k tokens, fp8 KV cache: serves users from 4× B300 SXM (1 model on 4), or 8× H200 SXM 141GB (1 model on 8).
With 4× B300 SXM (1 model on 4): 50 users guaranteed, 90 likely, at 39 tok/s (20 to 58, ±48%, estimated) each; reading
128k tokens: 3.0 s (1.1 to 4.9 s, ±63%).
Scenario "documents": each request brings 64k new tokens to read and generates 500; at least 20 tok/s per user. For a
chat (the conversation stays in the cache) use --prompt-cache 80.

Users: total = concurrent requests ÷ 10% active at once (--active); each gets ≥20 tok/s and the first token within 10 s

cache holds).
Accuracy: exact = computed; measured = this model on this card; calibrated = fitted on measurements of this card or of

to decide = ±50% or more, or a case not covered.
