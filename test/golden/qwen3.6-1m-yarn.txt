Summary
  Recommendation: none reliable — the speed is not reliable here (beyond the model's maximum context (262,144 tokens):
      the engine needs a RoPE / YaRN override, speed not reliable). The estimate, not to plan on: 1 server with 2×
      NVIDIA B200 SXM serves 10 users guaranteed, 20 likely, with requests of up to 1M tokens (≈ 790,000 words ≈ 1,600
      pages; 512k on average).
  Warning: 1M tokens is more than the model's own maximum (256k): the engine needs a context extension (YaRN) to start,
      which the lab documents up to 1,010,000 tokens.
  Not enough: RTX PRO 6000 Server, L4, L40S and A100, even with 8 GPUs in one server; RTX A6000, RTX 6000 Ada, RTX PRO
      6000 WS, RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q and consumer cards, even with 4; GH200, even with 2.
  Speed for each user: writing about 37 words per second (18 to 56; 49 tokens/s); with everyone busy the first words
      appear after about 10.4 s, and a full 1M-token request alone is read in about 39.0 s (1 token ≈ 0.75 words).
  Confidence: memory high (±6.6%); writing speed low, not precise enough to decide (more than ±50%); reading time low,
      not precise enough to decide (more than ±50%); user count low, not precise enough to decide (more than ±50%). Do
      not plan on these user counts: the speed is not reliable here (see the recommendation above).

Technical detail
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 1M tokens, fp8 KV cache: serves users from 2× B200 SXM (1 model on 2). With 2× B200 SXM (1 model on
2): 10 users guaranteed, 20 likely, at 49 tok/s (24 to 74, ±52%, not precise enough to decide) each; reading 1M tokens:
39.0 s (16.9 to 61.1 s, ±57%).
Scenario "documents": each request brings 512k new tokens to read and generates 500; at least 20 tok/s per user. For a
chat (the conversation stays in the cache) use --prompt-cache 80.
Note: 1M tokens is beyond the model's own maximum (256k): the engine refuses it without a RoPE override (YaRN, e.g. vLLM
--hf-overrides), and the quality beyond the context the lab declares is not guaranteed.

memory (KV cache) are computed exactly, the engine's fixed costs are estimated. Margin = room left for more
conversations after that request. Card memory = what CUDA reports (the engine's usable memory; nvidia-smi shows a little
more).
Users: total = concurrent requests ÷ 10% active at once (--active); each gets ≥20 tok/s and the first token within 10 s
(or, if longer, twice the wait for the first words of one typical request alone: half the context, less what the prefix
cache holds).
Accuracy: exact = computed; measured = this model on this card; calibrated = fitted on measurements of this card or of
the same model type (N points); estimated = calibrated on other cards, not verified, or extrapolated; not precise enough
to decide = ±50% or more, or a case not covered.
